# Code of conduct

We want DENIS to be a place where people of any background can contribute, ask questions and disagree without
being belittled. This policy follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (version 2.1).

## Our standard

Welcome and expected: being respectful, giving and receiving feedback graciously, focusing on what is best for
the project and its users, acknowledging mistakes, and assuming good faith.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal or political attacks, publishing
other people's private information, sexualised language or attention, and other conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not fit this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or
harmful.

Report a problem to the maintainers through the repository (a private report via the Security tab works for this
too). Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters will not face retaliation.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in
public.
